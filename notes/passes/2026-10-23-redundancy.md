## Considered and kept

- **§4's last two paragraphs** both close the section: "So ordinary Head's condition is of a kind the grammar already has …" and "Nothing in the form of Mod–Head expressions, then, shows the modifier function."
  - Two closers in a row is the signal the procedure names. But the first states a consequence (fusion's derivation "saves less than PHP suggest").
  - The second returns the section to its own question after a stretch on Det–Head's gap.
- **§3's forward pointer** ("Section 7 finds that kind of evidence in fused relatives …") and §7's closing of the same loop. Each is short, and each serves its own section.
- **§3's "So fusion isn't necessary for the position" and the next paragraph's opening.** They form the necessary/sufficient pair, so this is argument structure, not repetition.
- **§7's last paragraph against §1's preview of it** ("Fusion gets all three from the rules for relative clauses with a relative phrase"). This is the section's conclusion and its preview; the genre expects both.
- **"That's one fact favouring one analysis over two rivals, not a second fact for fusion"** (§3). Brett chose this wording on 23 Oct (option b).

# Evidence index: Fusion of functions revisited
<!-- SUMMARY: the record behind each corpus claim in the restructured paper (§§2–7 as drafted 18 Oct 2026), with links into the shared evidence repository; the paper links here once, from §2's note on data · status: current for the 18 Oct drafts; one record not yet pushed (see below) · updated: 2026-10-18 -->

This paper keeps no corpus data of its own. Every record is in the evidence repository shared with the companion paper on the categorization of determinatives: <https://github.com/BrettRey/determinatives-as-nouns>. Each link below goes to one record in that repository.

**What a record contains.** It gives the corpus, the query string, the date, the counts, and the label assigned to each line, with the lines the paper quotes. Some records also include the full concordance lines; for the searches Brett Reynolds ran by hand on 15–18 October, the full displays are kept privately, and the records give the counts and the lines relied on.

**How the lines were gathered and labelled.** Brett ran the searches by hand on english-corpora.org (COCA, NOW, iWeb). Claude (Anthropic; the model is named in each record) classified the lines, and each line's label is kept so that the classification can be checked. The genitive lines for §3 were labelled twice, in separate runs, the second without access to the first's labels, and the two runs found the same genitives. Judgments not attributed to a source are Brett's. On 18 October 2026 Brett checked by hand the 106 labels that the counts in §§3 and 7 depend on, and changed five (all in the sentence-initial *whomever* set; see the whoever-whomever-gaps-and-matrix record).

Section numbers are those of the restructured paper. §5 takes its examples from Payne et al. (2007); its corpus claims are a sentence and two footnotes on the adjective, and a footnote on the genitive.
